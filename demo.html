How this demo works. The seven appliance sounds are synthesised from scratch in your browser with the Web Audio API: motor hum as a fundamental plus harmonics, bearing friction as high-frequency noise bursts, imbalance as slow amplitude modulation. Each plan diagnosis is computed from that audio by a radix-2 FFT measuring high-frequency energy and envelope modulation depth, with thresholds calibrated against the 240-clip dataset used to train the real scikit-learn model. The microphone tool doesn't reuse those synthetic thresholds; instead it measures two noise-tolerant signatures that hold up on real audio: the spectral peakiness of the 2.5-9 kHz band (tonal grinding versus smooth airflow) and the 1-8 Hz amplitude-modulation depth (mechanical wobble). It checks both against reference profiles for the appliance you pick. Nothing leaves this device.
